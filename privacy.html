How we handle the personal information you share with us.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by [REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME] (registration number [REGISTRATION NUMBER]), trading as AquaFlow Plumbing, a plumbing business in Johannesburg, South Africa. We are the "responsible party" for your information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Our Information Officer is [INFORMATION OFFICER NAME]. You can contact them at privacy@example.com or at [PHYSICAL OR POSTAL ADDRESS].

What information we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • your name
  • your phone number
  • your email address
  • the service you need and your message (which may include your address or details about your property)

You don't have to use the form; you can phone us instead. But we need these details to reply to an enquiry sent through the form, so the fields are required.

If you contact us on WhatsApp, your messages and phone number are handled by WhatsApp (owned by Meta) under its own privacy policy, and we use them in the same way as form enquiries.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry: to contact you, give you a quote and arrange any work you ask for. We will not sell your information, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list without asking you first.

Who receives it

Your enquiry goes to AquaFlow Plumbing. To deliver it, the form uses an online service called Formspree, which receives your details and emails them to us. Formspree stores information on servers outside South Africa and is required to keep it secure. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months so we can follow up on quotes and jobs, then delete them. If you become a customer, we may need to keep job and invoice records for longer, for example for tax purposes.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us, free of charge, to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • delete your information, if we no longer need it or have no legal reason to keep it
  • stop using your information, or object to how we use it

To make a request, email privacy@example.com with your name and the phone number or email you used to contact us. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforeg.org.za.
